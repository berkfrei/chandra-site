# Project Brief — Chandra Frei

## What we are building
A doula booking and content site for Chandra Frei. Visitors can read about her approach, submit a contact inquiry, and book intake calls. Built on Next.js + Supabase + Stripe; deploys to Vercel.

## Who it is for
Pregnant people and their partners researching doulas. Mostly first-time bookings, often second or third trimester. Mode of mind is curiosity and a little anxiety — the writing should land calmly.

## What they need to do
1. Book a 20-minute intake call from any page
2. Read about Chandra and her offerings
3. Submit a deposit or payment for confirmed sessions
4. Read journal-style content (essays, photo notes)

## References
Editorial / ritual aesthetic — see the recent commits ("Rework home page into an editorial composition", "Map about chapters to a descending journey"). Variable rhythm, generous typography, subtle imagery.

## Out of scope (v1)
- Multi-doula support — Chandra solo for now
- Public testimonials beyond what she manually approves
- Calendar sync beyond the intake call flow
- Mobile app

## Tone
Editorial. Calm. Embodied.
